## The question
One sentence. If it takes three, the decision has not been separated from its context yet.

## Stakes
What is won and what is lost. What is reversible and at what cost.

## Timeline
When this must be decided, and what forces that date. If nothing forces it, say so, because
"decide later with better information" is then a real option.

## Success
How you would know, twelve months out, that this went well. Be concrete enough to be wrong about.

## Named stakeholder
The person the Liberator evaluates for. One name. If two people genuinely need separate advocacy,
run two Liberators rather than blending them into a household average.
